We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled \textbf{``Quantum-Enhanced Agrivoltaic Digital Twin: Spectral Co-Design, Multifunctional Net Energy Benefit, and Quantum-Secured Telemetry''} for consideration as a Research Article in \textit{Applied Energy}. It is not under consideration elsewhere; the journal's systems-level energy--agriculture--water scope is its natural home.

\noindent\textbf{Significance of the work}

Agrivoltaic deployment is constrained by a system-level trade-off: static semi-transparent photovoltaic modules resolve the food--energy land conflict only by uniformly sacrificing either electrical output or crop productivity. Using numerically exact quantum dynamics (PT-HOPS/SBD), we show that an organic photovoltaic layer integrated with a nanoparticle-on-mirror (NPoM) plasmonic cavity can \emph{actively} shape the transmitted spectrum to protect quantum coherence in the photosynthetic machinery, while the same cavity enables label-free SERS diagnostics of crop health. The digital twin links these quantum effects to canopy yields, Net Ecological Benefit, and cooperative economics.

\noindent\textbf{Key advances}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Coherence protection:} dual-band filtering at \qty{750}{\nm} and \qty{820}{\nm} extends exciton coherence by \qty{50}{\percent} and raises the forward transfer yield by \qty{25}{\percent} at room temperature.
\item \textbf{Experimental testability:} all modelled observables are anchored to independent experimental benchmarks, with two validation protocols (dual-band 2DES with SLM pulse shaping; time-resolved NPoM SERS).
\item \textbf{SERS--transport trade-off resolved:} the NPoM diverts 79--84\,\% of the excitation to the plasmon mode, but confined to a \qty{1}{\percent} sentinel area, the \qty{91.8}{\percent} local transport penalty costs 0.9 canopy-yield points (\num{0.980} to \num{0.971}; \qty{97.1}{\percent} preserved) while adding single-complex SERS diagnostics to the same footprint.
\item \textbf{Farm-scale proof of concept:} a \qty{500}{\m\squared} cooperative greenhouse in Cameroon combines \qty{90}{\MWh\per\yr} of OPV electricity, \qty{28}{\percent} evapotranspiration savings, a Net Ecological Benefit of \qty{19.6}{\kg\of{\ce{CO2e}}\per\m\squared\per\yr}, and a \qty{4.23}{\yr} payback under \qty{30}{\percent} subsidy.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Why \textit{Applied Energy}?}

The journal's readership spans photovoltaics, agricultural-energy systems, the water--energy--food nexus, lifecycle assessment, and energy economics --- precisely the scales this work integrates.

\noindent\textbf{Novelty, compliance, and declarations.} The integration of spectral coherence protection, NPoM SERS diagnostics, carbon-financed cooperative economics, and QKD-secured IoT in one platform is, to our knowledge, unreported; specifically, we report the first (i) agrivoltaic stack whose spectral architecture is co-designed by exact open-quantum-system dynamics, jointly optimizing excitation transport and power conversion, (ii) monolithic photothermal--PV--SERS device concept with field-relevant calibration in a single footprint, and (iii) multifunctional techno-economic and lifecycle framework internalizing heat, crop, water, sensing, and data-security trade-offs (\$/W $+$ \$/kg $+$ \$/m$^3$). Deployment barriers are made explicit: economic (tractable --- local materials halve CAPEX) versus physical (nanofabrication, fouling, encapsulation lifetime). The manuscript has been checked by a native English speaker with expertise in the field; all authors approved it, declare no competing interests, and are available to review at least three other \textit{Applied Energy} articles this year. An earlier version was considered at \textit{Nature Energy}; we submit directly here as the more suitable venue. Subscription publication (no APC due); the corresponding author is based in Cameroon.
