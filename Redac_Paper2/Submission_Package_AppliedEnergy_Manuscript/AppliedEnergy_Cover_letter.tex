\opening{Dear Editor,}

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled \textbf{``Spectral Co-Design of Agrivoltaic Modules for Multifunctional Net Energy Benefit: A Quantum-Guided Digital Twin''} for consideration as a Research Article in \textit{Applied Energy}. It is not under consideration elsewhere. The journal's systems-level treatment of energy conversion, the water--energy--food nexus, lifecycle assessment, and deployment economics is the natural home for this work.

\noindent\textbf{Central question and significance.}
Can spectral co-design guided by non-Markovian quantum dynamics deliver a positive multifunctional net energy benefit (power $+$ water $+$ carbon $+$ diagnostics) in a field-constrained agrivoltaic stack, and which barriers remain physical rather than economic? Static semi-transparent modules resolve the food--energy land conflict only by uniformly sacrificing electrical output or crop productivity. We show that an organic photovoltaic layer coupled to a nanoparticle-on-mirror (NPoM) cavity can actively shape the transmitted spectrum, protect vibronic coherence in a model photosynthetic complex, and host label-free SERS diagnostics---while a digital twin maps these device-level choices onto canopy yield, FAO-56 evapotranspiration, Net Ecological Benefit (NEB), and cooperative payback.

\noindent\textbf{Headline results (all modeled, fully traceable).}
\begin{itemize}
\item Dual-band transmission at \qtylist{750;820}{\nm} extends exciton coherence by \qty{50}{\percent} and raises forward transfer yield by \qty{25}{\percent}.
\item Confining the NPoM sentinel to \qty{1}{\percent} of canopy area absorbs a \qty{91.8}{\percent} local transport penalty while preserving global device-level yield $\Phi = \num{0.971}$ (vs.\ passive baseline $0.980$).
\item The smart shield cuts crop evapotranspiration by \qty{28}{\percent} (\qty{460}{\L\per\m\squared\per\yr}) and delivers a NEB of \qty{72.6}{\kg\of{\ce{CO2e}}\per\m\squared\per\yr}.
\item For a modeled \qty{500}{\m\squared} cooperative module in Cameroon, payback is \qty{4.32}{\yr} at \qty{30}{\percent} subsidy (\qty{2.68}{\yr} with local materials).
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Why \textit{Applied Energy}?}
The manuscript integrates photovoltaics, microclimate (FAO-56), lifecycle carbon accounting, cooperative techno-economics, and secure telemetry into a single decision-relevant framework---precisely the multi-scale, applied scope of the journal. Quantum methods appear only as the design engine for the spectral architecture; the value chain (what the quantum layer buys, and what it costs) is stated explicitly for an energy-systems readership.

\noindent\textbf{Novelty and positioning.}
To our knowledge, this is the first agrivoltaic stack whose spectral architecture is co-designed with numerically exact open-quantum-system dynamics, the first quantification of the local-to-global transport trade-off that makes a plasmonic diagnostic layer viable at canopy scale, and the first multifunctional TEA/LCA that internalizes power, water, carbon, and sensing in a single NEB and payback account. Economic barriers are shown to be tractable; physical barriers (nanofabrication, fouling, encapsulation) are stated honestly.
