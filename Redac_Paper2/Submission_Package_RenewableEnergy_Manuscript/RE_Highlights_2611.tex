\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Semi-transparent organic canopies are tested for three Cameroon perennial crops.
  \item Shade lifts dry-season yields by 8.8--30.2\% but costs light in the monsoon.
  \item Annual yields fall for coffee and tea; white pepper alone stays positive.
  \item Continuous sunlight makes quantum coherence effects numerically negligible.
  \item Matching silicon economics needs module cost at or below 15~USD/m$^2$.
\end{itemize}
